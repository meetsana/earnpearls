# Growth, Content, and SEO Plan

## Objective

Grow a qualified, trusting member base in approved countries without misleading earnings
claims or attracting traffic that violates provider agreements. SEO/content is the durable
primary channel; paid/referral/partner channels require separate economics and fraud limits.

## Audience and intent

Core intents:

- understand how survey rewards and validation work;
- compare transparent reward-status experiences;
- learn safe account and payout practices;
- find country-eligible survey guidance;
- solve pending/reversal/withdrawal questions;
- understand points and exact USD value.

Do not target vulnerable users with guaranteed-income, employment, instant-cash, or debt-
relief claims. Do not create country pages until actual provider/payout/legal availability is
approved for that country.

## Content pillars

1. Reward transparency: pending, validation, maturity, reversal, withdrawal.
2. Survey education: eligibility, honest qualification, time, devices, provider process.
3. Safety: account security, scams, privacy, support, payout credential protection.
4. Practical guides: profile completion, notifications, wallet history, support evidence.
5. Platform updates: feature/country/provider changes with dates and limitations.
6. Responsible money context: modest rewards and budgeting without income promises.

Each brief includes target intent, approved country, factual source, primary/secondary query,
unique value, internal links, schema type, CTA, legal/provider review needs, owner, update date,
and success/stop measure.

## Technical SEO

- unique title/description and one canonical HTTPS URL per public page;
- Open Graph/Twitter metadata and licensed/approved images;
- Organization/WebSite/FAQ/Article/Breadcrumb schema only when visible content supports it;
- database sitemap for core + due published CMS/blog URLs;
- `robots.txt` excludes authenticated/admin routes; staging sends `noindex, nofollow`;
- semantic headings, descriptive links, alt text, keyboard access, and readable contrast;
- 301/canonical strategy for renamed content; never index duplicate filter/search URLs;
- fast hashed static assets, optimized images, responsive layout, no blocking tracker by default.

Target p75 Core Web Vitals after production measurement: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms,
CLS ≤ 0.1. SEO release is blocked on accidental staging indexability, broken canonical,
fake schema/reviews, or materially misleading claim.

## Editorial workflow

1. Research intent and current factual sources; identify unsupported claims.
2. Draft in CMS as `draft`, with excerpt, metadata, canonical, category/tags/author.
3. Product/provider/legal review as applicable.
4. Accessibility, links, spelling, mobile preview, and structured-data review.
5. Schedule or publish; operations worker records due publication.
6. Inspect sitemap/canonical and internal links.
7. Review performance, support questions, accuracy, and decay; update creates a revision.
8. Archive/redirect content that becomes unavailable or false.

Final terms/privacy/cookies and provider/logo claims require their designated owner; content
capability alone cannot approve them. Testimonials remain labeled placeholders until genuine
consent and evidence exist.

## Internal linking model

- homepage → how it works, reward status guide, security, FAQ, register;
- survey guides → reward status, account safety, FAQ, relevant product route after sign-in;
- wallet/withdrawal guides → FAQ/support, not an availability promise;
- platform updates → canonical policy/help pages and dated affected feature;
- blog category/index → related posts based on genuine topic tags, not keyword stuffing.

## Lifecycle communication

Transactional security/reward/support/withdrawal messages follow member preferences and
event truth. Marketing email requires explicit opt-in, accurate sender, unsubscribe handling,
country/provider compliance, frequency limits, and suppression after opt-out. Database email
templates are versioned/audited; controlled mailbox tests precede material edits.

## Measurement

Only add production analytics after consent/privacy/processor approval. Minimum useful set:

- non-personal search impressions/clicks and indexed/canonical coverage;
- landing → eligible registration → verified account funnel;
- content-assisted survey engagement and support deflection;
- qualified/validated reward rate by approved source/country;
- unsubscribe/complaint/spam rate;
- Core Web Vitals and public error rate;
- content freshness, broken links, and support themes.

Use cohorts and contribution quality, not vanity page views. Do not combine personal survey
responses with marketing profiles unless contracts, consent, purpose, security, and retention
explicitly authorize it.

## Experiments

Every test has hypothesis, audience/country, primary and guardrail metrics, duration/sample
rule, implementation owner, stop criteria, and archived result. Prohibited experiments:
fake scarcity, preselected consent, hidden fees, misleading payout availability, difficult
unsubscribe, or changing authoritative financial values client-side.

## 90-day launch content sequence

- Pre-beta: trust/about, status guide, security guide, FAQ, platform limitations.
- Closed beta: anonymized support-derived guides, provider-approved survey education,
  transparent release notes.
- Open beta: country-specific content only for proven availability, genuine consented stories,
  comparison/education based on documented facts.
- Ongoing: monthly accuracy review for high-risk payout/legal/provider pages; quarterly topic
  and technical audit.
