# EarnPearls User Manual

## What EarnPearls is

EarnPearls helps eligible members find approved surveys and track rewards. Survey
availability and qualification are not guaranteed. Rewards may require provider validation
and maturity before withdrawal. Points are always shown with their USD value; any local
currency is only a dated estimate.

## Create and secure an account

Choose Register, enter your real email, display name, strong unique password, and one of the
currently listed countries. Open the verification email and use the link before it expires.
Never share a verification/reset link or password—even with support.

Sign in from Login. Security shows active sessions; revoke anything unfamiliar. Changing or
resetting a password invalidates other access. If a request is rate-limited, wait for the
shown period instead of repeating it.

## Dashboard

Dashboard summarizes pending, validated, mature, and withdrawable value; lifetime/weekly/
monthly earnings; eligible surveys; rank; recent transactions and notifications; support;
profile completion; and announcements. A value in Pending is not available cash.

## Surveys

Use filters for category, difficulty, device, and sorting. Open detail to review reward,
estimated time, eligibility, and validation note. Start launches the provider’s approved
flow and records a participation. Starting or finishing a provider screen does not itself
guarantee reward—provider confirmation determines validation.

History shows status. If confirmation is delayed, use the explanation and wait for the
provider window before opening support. Include the non-secret participation/reference ID;
never send passwords or full payout data.

## Wallet and reward states

- **Pending:** activity recorded, confirmation incomplete.
- **Validated:** provider/reconciliation evidence accepted.
- **Mature:** approved validation/settlement window completed.
- **Withdrawable:** eligible to reserve if withdrawals and a method are enabled.
- **Reserved:** attached to a withdrawal under review.
- **Paid:** payout completed with a reference.
- **Rejected/Reversed:** evidence did not qualify or was reversed; history remains visible.

Wallet history explains source, date, points, USD, current state, estimate, and notes. The
ledger is append-only so corrections appear as new evidence rather than hidden edits.

## Withdrawals

If withdrawals are unavailable, EarnPearls will not collect a destination. When enabled,
choose an available country-supported method, review minimum/fee/processing estimate, enter
the required destination, and submit once. Repeating the same request uses idempotency to
avoid duplicates.

History shows request/status/estimate/completion/reference/support. A rejection returns the
reserved points according to the ledger. Contact support if an estimate has passed; never
include the full destination in a message.

## Notifications and preferences

Notification Center supports unread, read, archived, and deleted states. Profile preferences
control reward, withdrawal, support, platform, and marketing delivery by channel. Security
notices remain enabled to protect the account. Marketing email is opt-in.

## Leaderboards

Enabled boards show display name, rank, metric, current period, and history. Administrators
may exclude accounts for privacy/integrity without changing wallet rewards. Rankings are for
healthy competition and do not guarantee extra reward unless a published campaign says so.

## Support

Read FAQs first, then create a category/subject/message ticket. Track status and conversation
from Support Center. Reply in the existing ticket for the same issue. Attachments are not
available until secure scanning is enabled.

Support will never ask for your password, verification/reset token, session cookie, full
payout destination, or provider secret. Revoke sessions and open a Security ticket if you
suspect unauthorized access.

## Profile and privacy

Update display name, timezone, currency preference, bio, and communication choices. Country
and email changes are restricted because they affect eligibility/security. Account archival
and privacy requests follow support/legal procedure so required financial/audit evidence is
preserved safely.

## Common messages

- **Feature unavailable:** the module is safely disabled; direct links will not bypass it.
- **Maintenance:** temporary platform work; administrators retain recovery access.
- **Access denied:** account/Limit Template does not have that capability.
- **Offline/network:** your request may not have reached the server; check history before
  retrying a financial action.
- **Request ID:** safe reference to give support with timestamp and steps.

## Truthful expectations

EarnPearls is not employment and does not promise income, qualification, survey volume, or
continuous payout availability. Never pay anyone who claims they can unlock your account or
guarantee rewards. Use only the official HTTPS site supplied by the owner.
