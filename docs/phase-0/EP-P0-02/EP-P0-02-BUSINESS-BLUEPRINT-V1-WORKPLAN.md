## Chapter contract

Every chapter must contain:

1. purpose and business outcome;
2. exact EP-P0-01 requirement IDs;
3. exact Constitution citations where the source materially controls the text;
4. assumptions separated from facts;
5. ODR, DDR, EFE, and OCR dependencies;
6. owner decisions still required;
7. measurable operating indicators;
8. risk, fraud, security, and compliance implications where applicable;
9. V1 versus Future separation; and
10. evidence and approval checklist.

## Controlled production sequence

1. Build the evidence ledger and decision-dependency matrix.
2. Draft Constitution-controlled chapters that do not require external facts.
3. Draft conditional sections around open ODRs without selecting an answer.
4. Add market and competitor analysis only from cited current evidence.
5. Reconcile every chapter against EP-P0-01 IDs and the 16-field register.
6. Render the complete document and verify page count, navigation, tables, and
   cross-references.
7. Run independent semantic, financial, compliance, and operational review.
8. Leave the status at `REVISION COMPLETED — PENDING INDEPENDENT REVIEW` until
   the review is complete.

## Immediate work package

The first active package is the evidence ledger plus ODR dependency matrix. No
frontend implementation is needed during this package. The current UI may be
inventoried in parallel, but it cannot become the design baseline until EP-P0-04
and EP-P0-05 are approved.
