## Executive Summary — controlled first draft

EarnPearls is a global-rewards-platform concept whose focused Version 1 centers
on surveys, wallet transparency, rewards, user experience, trust, and scalable
expansion. It is not defined as merely a survey website. The intended product
qualities are modern, scalable, secure, trustworthy, and highly optimized.

The public identity is EarnPearls with the tagline “Your Time. Your Rewards.” The
platform uses points internally and USD as the accounting source of truth. Local
currency is display-only. Version 1 is English-only while keeping future
multilingual support technically possible.

The launch-country list and eligibility rules are not operationally final. The
Constitution supplies named target and excluded markets, while ODR-003 and
EFE-003 control the final country matrix, legal basis, age/eligibility rules, and
activation process. The blueprint must therefore distinguish constitutional
direction from approved operating policy.

EarnPearls revenue, provider economics, user rewards, platform margin, withdrawal
methods, provider selection, and provider visibility are not approved business
facts yet. ODR-005, ODR-014, ODR-016, ODR-018, ODR-027, EFE-001, EFE-002, and
EFE-010 must be resolved or evidenced before the final blueprint can present an
operational financial model.

The platform’s financial lifecycle is event-driven. Pending provider earnings do
not become Validated merely because time passes. Provider confirmation or
verified reconciliation is required; later maturity and withdrawability depend
on provider-specific settlement, cleared funds, and configured risk conditions.

This blueprint is a planning artifact, not implementation authorization. The
existing repository UI remains an exploratory prototype until the Product
Blueprint, UI/UX review, architecture, security, database, API, infrastructure,
and implementation-roadmap gates are approved.
